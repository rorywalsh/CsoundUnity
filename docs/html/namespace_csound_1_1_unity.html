◆ AudioPath

enum Csound.Unity.AudioPath

Selects which audio path CsoundUnity uses to deliver audio.

Enumerator
OnAudioFilterRead 

Classic Unity audio path via OnAudioFilterRead. Works on all supported Unity versions. Audio is routed through the AudioSource and AudioMixer graph.

IAudioGenerator 

Unity 6+ IAudioGenerator path. Drives the AudioSource directly — avoids the resampling step and integrates cleanly with the new Unity Audio system. Audio passes through the AudioMixer graph. Requires an AudioSource component.

RootOutput 

Unity 6+ RootOutput path. Bypasses the AudioMixer entirely — audio is mixed additively into Unity's main output. No AudioSource required; 3D spatialization and mixer effects are not applied. Ideal when the mixer is not needed or for multi-channel output beyond the AudioSource's channel count.
