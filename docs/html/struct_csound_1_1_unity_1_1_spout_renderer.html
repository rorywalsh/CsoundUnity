Detailed Description

Drives Csound for one Unity audio block and copies spout into the block's buffer. The two Unity 6 audio paths, AudioPath.IAudioGenerator and AudioPath.RootOutput, both consist of this: they differ in where Unity hands the buffer to them, not in what they do with it, so they embed this and keep their own plumbing.

Unmanaged and blittable, like the two structs that hold it: Unity stores them in its own audio memory between invocations, which is also what makes the three fields below persist.
