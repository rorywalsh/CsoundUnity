Detailed Description

A clip's next unscheduled ordinal on the master's beat grid. Pattern length changes only the lane index, never the grid. This value is copied into the scheduler checkpoint on rebuilds.

Take the next due step and its delay in seconds, advancing the cursor only on success.

Parameters
frameThe master's beat, tempo and time, sampled once for this evaluation.
stepBeatsHow many beats one step lasts, which is the grid itself.
countHow many steps the pattern has. It gives the lane index, not the grid.
lookaheadHow far ahead, in seconds, a step may be taken.
remainingSecondsHow much of the clip is left, so nothing is taken past its end.
stepThe step that came due, as an index into the pattern.
delayHow long from now, in seconds, that step has to sound.
clipAgeSecondsClip-local time, used to recover a beat at the clip's entry.
