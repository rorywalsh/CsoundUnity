◆ ArpDirection

enum Csound.Unity.Utilities.ArpDirection

Direction for an arpeggio or melodic pattern.

Enumerator
Up 

Plays notes in ascending pitch order, looping back to the lowest after the top.

Down 

Plays notes in descending pitch order, looping back to the highest after the bottom.

UpDown 

Bounce: ascends to the top then descends back to the bottom, then repeats.

Random 

Selects a random pitch from the array on each step.
