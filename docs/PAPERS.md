# Papers on CsoundUnity

Papers and theses on the package and projects built with it.

## 2015

- Rory Walsh, *[Csound and Unity3D](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.50369)*, ICSC 2015. The first paper about this wrapper.

## 2019

- Juliana Quintas, *[Procedural Audio Generation Applied to Digital Games](https://web.ipca.pt/symposium/2021/)*, master’s thesis, IPCA, 2019. [Prototype source](https://github.com/julianaquintas/master-thesis)

## 2021

- Vadim Nickel, *[Conception and Evaluation of a Procedural Music System for Engagement with Player Emotion and Memory in Digital Games](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354552162_Conception_and_Evaluation_of_a_Procedural_Music_System_for_Engagement_with_Player_Emotion_and_Memory_in_Digital_Games)*, master’s thesis, TH Köln, 2021.

## 2022

- Mateo Larrea and Caio Jiacomini, *[New Utility Classes and Sketches for Developers and Sound Designers in CsoundUnity](https://csound.com/icsc2022/proceedings/New%20Utility%20Classes%20and%20Sketches%20for%20Developers%20and%20Sound%20Designers%20in%20CsoundUnity.pdf)*, ICSC 2022.
- Pedro Sodre, *[Designing VR Applications with CsoundUnity](https://csound.com/icsc2022/proceedings/Designing%20VR%20Applications%20with%20CsoundUnity.pdf)*, ICSC 2022.
- Satoshi Koibuchi and Akinori Ito, *[Interactive sound effect generate system for “buechu-buechu” sound with CsoundUnity](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/wiieej/21.04/0/21.04_37/_article/-char/en)*, Expressive Japan 2022.
